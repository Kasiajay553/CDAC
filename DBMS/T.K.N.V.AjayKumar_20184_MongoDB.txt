Assignment 3 – Sorting and Salary Range
* Display all employee records in sorted order of Employee Name.
db.company.find().sort({'Employe_Name' : 1});

* Display all records in descending order of salary.
db.company.find().sort({'salary' : -1});

* Find employees with salary between ₹5,000 and ₹10,000.
db.company.find({
  'salary':{
